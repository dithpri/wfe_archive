[i]There once was a land of kind, honest people who created a safe and prosperous society

This attracted greedy, jealous trolls who infiltrated the land, exploited their kindness and sought to destroy them

The peaceful nature of the people stopped them casting the trolls out and the situation looked hopeless

One day, a hero arrived from a nearby land. He chastised the weak leaders and promised to rid them of the usurious trolls

With a magic mist he removed the trolls. The people rejoiced, but before they could enjoy their freedom, Kingdoms still under the Troll&#39;s spell began to attack, unaware of who their true enemy was.

The hero perished and the land and neighbouring kingdoms forever fell under the evil spell of the greedy Trolls.[/i]
