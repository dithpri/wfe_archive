The Continental Bepis Tribunal (CBT) is a neutral diplomatic region established by treaty as a permanent forum for international diplomacy and composed of fully sovereign nations. The region possesses no common government beyond the administrative institutions necessary for its operation, and membership does not diminish the sovereignty or independence of its member states. Instead, the Bepis Archipelago serves as permanently neutral ground through which member nations communicate, negotiate treaties, discuss matters of international concern, and resolve disputes while remaining wholly independent in their domestic and foreign affairs.
