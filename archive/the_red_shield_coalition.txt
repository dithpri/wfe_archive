&amp;#9773; Overview
The Red Shield Coalition is an unyielding bastion of proletarian solidarity. We unite to resist capitalist exploitation, coordinate socialist economies, and defend our collective sovereignty against imperialist subversion.

Discipline: Absolute adherence to regional security directives.
