This is a world built from fragments - of history, of legend, of myth - where figures once thought confined to old books and older stories now govern real nations, in real time, in a present day much like any other. No one here asks how a warrior-king wields a smartphone, or how a goddess balances a national budget. It is simply true, the way gravity is true, the way the tide comes in. Terra Legenda does not explain itself. It only asks that you live in it.
