[b]The Dominion of Order[/b]

[hr]

The Dominion of Order is a region, based on the planet Dominion, where a constant flurry of activity can be seen all over its scenic landscapes. 

In the Dominion of Order, you can [i]wage wars, grow your nation, and climb the ranks to become a regional powerhouse[/i]. And, if you just want to kick back, relax, and watch the region grow, there&#146;s always a place for you on Dominion&#146;s sunny shores. 

Without further ado, welcome to the Dominion of Order. Any nation,
of any ideology, size or lore can join!!
