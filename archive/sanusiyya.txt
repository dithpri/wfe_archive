[b]&quot;Faith, Discipline, Unity.&quot;
[/b]
[b]Sanusiyya[/b] is a region founded upon the dynastic legacy of the [b]House of Senussi[/b], shaped by faith, discipline, and communal obligation. Its nations inherit traditions formed in [b]The Emirate of Cyranaica[/b] and later unified under [nation=long]Libbiya[/nation], where authority was understood as stewardship rather than excess.

Governance within [b]Sanusiyya[/b] emphasizes unity through duty and cooperation rooted in shared values, not transient ideology. Leadership is guided by moral responsibility, restraint, and historical continuity.

Nations who uphold faith, order, and collective strength are welcome to stand with Sanusiyya in continuity and resolve.
