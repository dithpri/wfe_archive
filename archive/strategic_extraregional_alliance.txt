[b]The Strategic Extraregional Alliance[/b]

The Strategic Extraregional Alliance is an interregional organization committed to strengthening cooperation between its member regions, protecting their sovereignty, and promoting peace, stability, and mutual support across NationStates.

Through communication, diplomacy, and cooperation, the Alliance works to advance the shared interests of its members and uphold the principles set out in its charter.

[b]Members:[/b] Virelion, New Virelia, and Urseom.
