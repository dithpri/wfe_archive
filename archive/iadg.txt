The International Alliance for Development and Growth (IADG) is a liberal and development-oriented regional alliance dedicated to building a prosperous, free, and cooperative international community.

The Alliance was founded on the belief that economic development, political liberty, and international cooperation are mutually reinforcing rather than competing objectives.

Rather than attempting to impose a single political or economic model upon its members, the IADG seeks to provide a framework in which nations can cooperate while maintaining their own sovereignty, cultures, institutions, and domestic policies.
