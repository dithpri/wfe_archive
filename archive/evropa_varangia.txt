[b]Evropa Varangia
[/b]
Evropa Varangia is a sovereign regional agreement linking northern Europe to the great Eurasian sphere. The region is constructed as an inclusive space for diverse governments, and serves as a platform for economic cooperation, security alignment and regional growth without forcing member states into a single political model.

The region respects the sovereignty of the states and promotes technological progress, a solid public development and a strong defensive readiness. Whether ruled by monarchies, republics or high-welfare technocracies, Evropa Varangia offers a neutral, high-capacity environment for all member states to grow their economies and maintain their independence.
