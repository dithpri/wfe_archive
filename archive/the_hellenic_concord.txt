Many States. One Heritage.

The Hellenic Concord is a regional community of nations united by a shared Hellenic heritage. Though its members share cultural roots, languages, traditions, mythology, architecture, and history, they differ greatly in their political systems, economic philosophies, and visions for the future.

The Concord does not seek to erase these differences. Instead, it celebrates them as different interpretations of what it means to inherit the Hellenic tradition.
