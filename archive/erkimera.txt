[region]Erkimera[/region] is an isolated region for nations that only want to interact with a small group of people. We avoid dealings with the World Assembly as we believe in self-governed isolation. Our members are free to govern themselves as they wish; we only moderate international relations and mediate conflicts between our members.

We value respect, and expect our members to treat others kindly. Officers reserve the right to eject/ban anyone for misconduct under their judgement.
