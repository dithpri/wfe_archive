Government and Political Structure

World Domination operates under a centralized global administrative system designed to coordinate governance across the entire region.

Form of Government: Global Unified Authority

Executive Branch: The Supreme Leader and Executive Council

Legislative Branch: Global Council

Judicial Branch: Unified Global Tribunal

Administrative Structure: Regional governments, continental administrations, and local authorities

Political System: Centralized administration

Official Governing Document: Global Charter of Domination

The government is responsible for maintaining public infrastructure, coordinating international and regional policy, managing natural resources, establishing common standards, and overseeing public services.
