The&nbsp;FIA Formula 3 Championship is a third-tier international&nbsp;single-seater racing&nbsp;championship organised by the&nbsp;[url=region=Federation internationale del automobil][b]FIA[/b][/url]. The championship launched in 2019 as a feeder series for [url=region=Formula One][b]Formula One[/b][/url]&nbsp;and&nbsp;the [url=region=FIA Formula 2 Championship][b]FIA Formula 2 Championship[/b][/url].
