The&nbsp;FIA Formula 3 Championship&nbsp;(FIA F3) is a third-tier international&nbsp;single-seater racing&nbsp;championship organised by the&nbsp;F&eacute;d&eacute;ration Internationale de l&#39;Automobile&nbsp;(FIA). The championship launched in 2019 as a feeder series for the&nbsp;FIA Formula 1 World Championship&nbsp;and&nbsp;FIA Formula 2 Championships. It was the result of a merger between two third-tier&nbsp;single-seater racing&nbsp;championships, the&nbsp;GP3 Series&nbsp;and the&nbsp;FIA Formula 3 European Championship. This championship is part of the&nbsp;FIA Global Pathway&nbsp;consolidation project plan. Unlike its co-predecessor, the Formula 3 European Championship, the series runs exclusively in support of Formula One races.
