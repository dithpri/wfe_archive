The&nbsp;Japanese Super Formula Championship&nbsp;is a&nbsp;formula racing&nbsp;series held primarily in&nbsp;Japan. It is considered to be the pinnacle of single-seater racing in Japan or&nbsp;Asia&nbsp;as a whole, making it one of the top motorsport series in the region. It is sanctioned by the [b][url=region=Japan Automobile Federation]JAF[/url][/b].
