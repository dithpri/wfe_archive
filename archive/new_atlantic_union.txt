The New Atlantic Union is a region built for nations that believe society is strongest when solidarity, public responsibility, and national self-determination exist side by side. It welcomes socialist, social-democratic, labor-oriented, and civic-national governments that seek to combine economic justice with strong institutions and national sovereignty.

The Union encourages cooperation in industry, infrastructure, healthcare, education, workers&#146; rights, and regional security. Member states maintain their own political systems and identities while working together to reduce inequality, strengthen public services, protect strategic industries, and promote long-term economic development.
