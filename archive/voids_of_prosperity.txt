The [b]Voids of Prosperity[/b] are known to be secluded, abundant with resources lands. They were first colonized by people of what is now known as Farsky, the bulwark of the region. However, it is subject for debate whether Farsky shields region from the outside, or the opposite.

The Voids seem to attract all sorts of rogue and ill-mannered nations, ready to challenge Farsky for the regional leadership. Many of them, on the other hand, attempt to direct such unregulated power for the common good.
