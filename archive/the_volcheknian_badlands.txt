The Volcheknian Badlands was inhabited by a motley collection of fallen nations: the United Collective of Volcheknia, its puppet state Fronchia, the freedom loving Borderlands of Lostasia, the strong and stoic Oppressed Peoples of Kuszleika, the eccentric Kingdom of Tazmosia, a peaceful nation of science obsessed androids, and a mysterious dark haze that consumed the horizon in the distance. The fiercely independent land of Hirech remains, maintaining the region and its history.
