The Cyrannus Star System is a group of four stars, in a double binary configuration. The four stars are denoted as Helios Alpha, Beta, Gamma, and Delta. Helios Alpha and Beta orbit each other at a distance of 63 SU from their barycenter. Helios Gamma and Delta orbit each other at a distance of 70 SU from their barycenter. 

The Twelve Colonies of Man was an interplanetary state founded here.
