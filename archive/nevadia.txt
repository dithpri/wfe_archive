[b][region]Nevadia[/region][/b] is an old region, originally founded by [nation]Vulxo[/nation]. Nevadia was turned into a frontier, and then the original founder and current [b]WA Delegate[/b] [nation]Vulxo[/nation] went inactive and was presumed dead. During this time, another nation gained the World Assembly Delegate position through new members of the Nevadian voting power, and the region was subsequently moved and merged elsewhere.

Now, [b]Nevadia is back.[/b]

The region is once again being rebuilt, with plans to welcome new nations and restore Nevadia as a home for its community.

Nevadia also maintains [b]strong historical and cultural ties with the [region]Nevadian Mandate[/region][/b], sharing a close Nevadian identity and longstanding connections.
