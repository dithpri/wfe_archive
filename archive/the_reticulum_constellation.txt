Reticulum is a small, faint constellation in the southern sky. Its name is Latin for a small net, or reticle&#151;a net of crosshairs at the focus of a telescope eyepiece that is used to measure star positions. Only two of the stars in this constellation are brighter than visual magnitude 5: Alpha (&amp;#945;) (the proper name is Rhombus since 19 September 2024) and Beta (&amp;#946;) reticuli.
