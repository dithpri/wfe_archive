    Situated in the harsh and soulless far regions of the northern hemisphere, S-PS is known for strong technological advancement, intense nationalism, and a lack of plumbing. Fecal matter does not need to be disposed of as it freezes immediately upon contact with the frigid outside air. This frozen doo-doo serves as the primary source of fuel in S-PS, and it powers our 6th generation technology with the highest efficiency the world has ever seen. 
    Its wildlife consists of gigantic predatory monstrosities that have evolved to survive in this environment by developing scat-storing energy organs that assist in heat retention.
