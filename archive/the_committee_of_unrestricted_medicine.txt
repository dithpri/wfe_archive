The Committee of Unrestricted Medicine, hailing from a dimension where the Hippocratic Oath was a forgotten myth, convened in their endless hall, their silken robes brushing against floors made of polished bone, and they smiled warmly at the latest volunteer, a nervous, frail woman clutching a jar of her own tears.

A room that was born of weirdness and unethical experimentations within the medical dimensional realm where the mad scientists and doctors congregate.
