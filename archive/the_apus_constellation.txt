Apus is a small constellation in the southern sky. It represents a bird-of-paradise, and its name means &quot;without feet&quot; in Greek because the bird-of-paradise was once wrongly believed to lack feet. The five brightest stars are all reddish in hue. Shading the others at apparent magnitude 3.8 is Alpha Apodis (formally named Paradys), an orange giant that has around 48 times the diameter and 928 times the luminosity of the Sun. Marginally fainter is Gamma Apodis, another aging giant star. Delta Apodis is a double star.
