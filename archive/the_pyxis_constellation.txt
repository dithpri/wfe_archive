Pyxis is a small and faint constellation in the southern sky. Abbreviated from Pyxis Nautica, its name is Latin for a mariner&#39;s compass. A faint constellation, its three brightest stars&#151;Alpha, Beta and Gamma Pyxidis&#151;are in a rough line. At magnitude 3.68, Alpha is the constellation&#39;s brightest star. It is a blue-white star approximately 880 light-years (270 parsecs) distant and around 22,000 times as luminous as the Sun.
