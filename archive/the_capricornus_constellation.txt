Capricornus is one of the constellations of the zodiac. Its name is Latin for &quot;horned goat&quot; or &quot;goat horn&quot; or &quot;having horns like a goat&#39;s&quot;, and it is commonly represented in the form of a sea goat: a mythical creature that is half goat, half fish. The brightest star in Capricornus is &amp;#948; Capricorni, also called Deneb Algedi, with a magnitude of 2.9, located 39 light-years from Earth.
