Harrenhal, the largest castle in the Seven Kingdoms, is in the riverlands, on the north shore of the Gods Eye. Since the burning of Harrenhal by dragonfire in Aegon&#39;s Conquest, however, the castle has become a dark and ruinous place.

Harrenhal&#39;s holdings are some of the richest in Westeros, claiming vast tracts of green fertile land which reach as far as the hills of House Wode near the crownlands. Harrentown is found near the castle.
