The Marinthos Isles are a vast archipelago once abandoned by a great empire, leaving its scattered communities to face decades of conflict and isolation. Over time, the island clans, merchant fleets, and coastal settlements forged peace through the Concord of Tides, creating a resilient and cooperative regional identity. Today, Marinthos stands as a unified archipelago shaped by maritime tradition, shared governance, and the determination of its people to thrive beyond the shadow of the past.
