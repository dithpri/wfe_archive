Coma Berenices is an ancient asterism in the northern sky, which has been defined as one of the 88 modern constellations. The constellation&#39;s major stars are Alpha, Beta, and Gamma Comae Berenices. They form a half square, along the diagonal of which run Berenice&#39;s imaginary tresses, formed by the Coma Star Cluster. The constellation&#39;s brightest star is Beta Comae Berenices, a 4.2-magnitude main sequence star similar to the Sun.
