[b]The United Patriots Coalition[/b], founded in 2026, is an umbrella coalition of right-leaning nations united by patriotism, national sovereignty, conservative values, and right-wing populism. The coalition promotes strong leadership, national identity, law and order, and cooperation among members while opposing left-wing ideologies and advocating governments that put their people and national interests first.[hr]
