Carina is a constellation in the southern sky. Its name is Latin for the keel of a ship, and it was the southern foundation of the larger constellation of Argo Navis (the ship Argo) until it was divided into three pieces, the other two being Puppis (the poop deck), and Vela (the sails of the ship). Carina contains Canopus, a white-hued bright giant that is the second-brightest star in the night sky at magnitude &amp;#8722;0.72. There are several other stars above magnitude 3 in Carina: Beta Carinae, traditionally called Miaplacidus; Epsilon Carinae (Avior) is an orange-hued giant star; another fairly bright star is the blue-white-hued Theta Carinae; Eta Carinae is the most prominent variable star in Carina.
