The Conservativing Nations
A harsh, 19th-century industrial plateau choked in yellow smog and soot-stained brick. Rejecting modern technology, it runs entirely on steam engines, coal, and heavy manual labor. Ruled by strict tradition, pious hierarchy, and wealthy industrialists, the populace fiercely defends its old ways with the motto: &#147;What was built by our fathers shall sustain our sons.&#148; It is a stubborn, roaring world of blast furnaces and unyielding pride that has locked its gates to the future.
