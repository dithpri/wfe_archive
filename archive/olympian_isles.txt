[i]Welcome to the Olympian Isles[/i]

Rising from the wine-dark seas, the Olympian Isles are an archipelago of sovereign city-states, maritime republics, and ancient empires. Forged in the shadow of towering marble peaks and blessed by the tides, our nations compete for glory in trade, diplomacy, and grand cultural games. Whether you rule a peaceful island sanctuary or a sprawling naval power, claim your shore and etch your legacy into legend.
