Orion is a prominent set of stars visible during winter in the northern celestial hemisphere. Orion is most prominent during winter evenings in the Northern Hemisphere, as are five other constellations that have stars in the Winter Hexagon asterism. Orion&#39;s two brightest stars, Rigel (&amp;#946;) and Betelgeuse (&amp;#945;), are both among the brightest stars in the night sky; both are supergiants and slightly variable. There are a further six stars brighter than magnitude 3.0, including three making the short straight line of the Orion&#39;s Belt asterism.
