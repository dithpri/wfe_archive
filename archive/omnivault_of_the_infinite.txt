Before the first star cooled, there was only the Omnivault, the hollow at creation&#146;s center from which all things first poured. The Architects did not build this place, they remembered it into being, drawing every nation that has ever risen from this single point of origin.

Here past and future fold into one instant. The Architects rule nothing by decree. They shepherd each new dominion into shape, then loose it among the stars, keeping their quiet watch over all that was, is, and shall be.
