[b]The Middle West[/b] is a vast region located in western Zebrica, stretching from the Great Saddle Desert to the eastern coasts of the Celestial Sea. Long regarded as the crossroads of Zebrica, it is home to countless kingdoms, city-states, tribal confederations, and ancient civilizations. Zebras make up the majority of the population, alongside significant communities of camels, gazelles, oryxes, griffons, and other native peoples, each contributing to the region&#39;s rich cultural tapestry.
