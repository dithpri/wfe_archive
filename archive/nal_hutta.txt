Nal Hutta, which translates to &quot;Glorious Jewel&quot; in Huttese, is a stagnant, swampy planet located in the Outer Rim&#39;s Hutt Space that serves as the capital of the Hutt crime empire.. Today, the planet&#39;s atmosphere is hot, humid, and constantly choked by an oily, greasy rain that precipitates from heavy industrial pollution and widespread strip-mining. Its surface is a treacherous landscape of fetid bogs, murky bayous, and toxic waste pools, broken up only by the grand, palace-like fortresses of the Hutt Cartel lords. Operating entirely outside the jurisdiction of galactic law, Nal Hutta acts as a massive hub for the galactic underworld, closely tied to its heavily urbanized smuggler moon, Nar Shaddaa.
