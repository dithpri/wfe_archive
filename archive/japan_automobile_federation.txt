The&nbsp;JAF is an automobile association based in&nbsp;Minato,&nbsp;Tokyo. Founded in 1963, the main purpose of the organization is to handle various businesses related to automobiles, and to protect the interests of owners and drivers in Japan. It is the only member of the [url=region=Federation Internationale del Automobil][b]FIA[/b][/url] in Japan.
