The Marklenstal family, holders of a modest banking fortune, fled Nazi Germany in 1937 for neutral Switzerland, and later emigrated to the United States. There, the eldest son, Heinrich Marklenstal, founded a small import-export firm. Through shrewd acquisitions during the post-war industrial boom, the firm grew into a diversified holding company. By the 1980s, Marklenstal Capital had evolved into a sovereign corporate entity, purchasing a small island territory to establish its own state &#151; a nation where capital is law, and every citizen knows their place.
