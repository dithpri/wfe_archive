The Sovereign Realm of Hermon is a fully self-contained nation founded on the core tenets of absolute state sovereignty, self-reliance, and strict non-interference. Hermon explicitly rejects all forms of supranational governance, foreign mandates, and international treaties that compromise domestic authority.
Operating under the Doctrine of Absolute Autarchy, Hermon maintains a completely inward-focused political model, prioritizing internal development, order, and complete independence from regional blocs.
