Chamaeleon is a small constellation in the deep southern sky. It is named after the chameleon, a kind of lizard. It was first defined in the end of the 16th century. There are four bright stars in Chamaeleon that form a compact diamond-shape approximately 10 degrees from the south celestial pole and about 15 degrees south of Acrux, along the axis formed by Acrux and Gamma Crucis: Alpha Chamaeleontis, Beta Chamaeleontis, Delta Chamaeleontis and the brighter star is Delta2 Chamaeleontis.
