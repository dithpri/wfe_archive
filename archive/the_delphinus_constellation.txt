Delphinus is a small constellation in the Northern Celestial Hemisphere, close to the celestial equator.  It is one of the smaller constellations, ranked 69th in size. Delphinus&#39; five brightest stars form a distinctive asterism symbolizing a dolphin with four stars representing the body and one the tail. Delphinus is a faint constellation with only two stars brighter than an apparent magnitude of 4, Beta Delphini (Rotanev) at magnitude 3.6 and Alpha Delphini (Sualocin) at magnitude 3.8.
