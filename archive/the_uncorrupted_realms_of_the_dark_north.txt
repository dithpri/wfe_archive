The Northern Wastes are vast and terrible. Endless expanses of ice and snow blanket the land for most of the year, and wild animals stalk the wilderness seeking their next meal with a ferocity that boarders on madness. Worse still, monsters lurk between the snowdrifts, hungrily eyeing the hovels where men and dwarves&#39; dwell. Yet the people here are hardy and stout, refusing to succumb to corruption, stubbornly resisting both their homelands treacherous climate and the predations of evil beings with every fiber of their beings. In the unforgiving lands of the Northern Wastes only those with the will to do whatever it takes have any hope of surviving, and thus far the Uncorrupted Realms have been more then equal to the challenge.
