[b]Marstal[/b] is the capital and largest city of [region]The Mood[/region], renowned for its centuries-old maritime heritage and colorful harbor. Known for its rich shipbuilding history, it features attractions like the Marstal Maritime Museum, the scenic Eriks Hale beach, and the unique white bathing houses.
