SEATO

Southeast Asia Treaty Organization

United in Defense. Resolute for Peace.

SEATO is an international security alliance committed to protecting the sovereignty, territorial integrity, and political independence of its member states.

Through collective defense, diplomatic cooperation, and coordinated security policies, SEATO seeks to deter aggression, maintain regional stability, and uphold international law.

Our Principles

Collective Security

Sovereign Equality

Mutual Defense

Peaceful Diplomacy

Regional Stability

SEATO stands united in the defense of its members, recognizing that regional peace is a shared responsibility and that every sovereign nation deserves security and independence.
