The League of Slavic Nations is an international community founded upon the principles of Slavic brotherhood, sisterhood, cooperation, and mutual respect.

All Slavic nations, regardless of their political systems, ideologies, traditions, or national interests, are welcome within the League. Whether large or small, monarchist or republican, democratic or otherwise, every nation has a place among its fellow Slavic brothers and sisters.

The League does not seek to undermine the sovereignty or independence of its members. Rather, it recognizes that every nation has the right to govern itself, preserve its own identity, and determine its own future.
