[b]Nichirin[/b], officially the [b]Miyamoto Shogunate[/b], is a country in Northern Valtheria. Bounded by the Hyperborean Ocean to the north and the Bay of Serica to the east, it borders Rudania to the south, Axerfreditenshin to the southeast, and Guandong to the east. With a population exceeding 348 million on its mainland and 134 million in its overseas territories, it is the third-most populous country. Mainland Nichirin is divided into 4 SARs (Special Administrative Regions), 47 prefectures, and 2 autonomous prefectures.
