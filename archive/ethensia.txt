[b]~&amp;#11089; Ethensia &amp;#11089;~[/b][i]
(Ethensian: Ethensiani), officially the [url=https://www.nationstates.net/nation=ethensia/detail=factbook/id=2578732]United Federal Commonwealth of Ethensia[/url], is an island country situated in the Northern Vowesi region in Coyphus. It has a population of around 20.4 million people, and consists of 7 self-governing states: Jandura, Abutakia, Buroda, Viola, Maybel, Tunouth, and Northern Ethensia.
