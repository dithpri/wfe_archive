Altea, a region dedicated to friendly and dedicated roleplay and world building between its members. In a Post Modern setting inspired by the likes of Ace Combat, Forever Winter, Metal Gear, and many more.  Altea takes place in a world of alternate cultures, politic, and geography. Focusing on interesting character roleplay and finer details while the world around rages on and on. Diplomacy with non member nations is allowed but most canon roleplay will take place between member nations.
