Crux is a constellation of the southern sky that is centred on four bright stars in a cross-shaped asterism commonly known as the Southern Cross. It lies on the southern end of the Milky Way&#39;s visible band. Blue-white &amp;#945; Crucis (Acrux) is the most southerly member of the constellation, and at magnitude 0.8, the brightest. The three other stars of the cross appear clockwise and in order of lessening magnitude: &amp;#946; Crucis (Mimosa), &amp;#947; Crucis (Gacrux), and &amp;#948; Crucis (Imai). &amp;#949; Crucis (Ginan) also lies within the cross asterism.
