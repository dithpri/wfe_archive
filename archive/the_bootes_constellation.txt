Bo&ouml;tes is a constellation in the northern sky, located between 0&deg; and +60&deg; declination, and 13 and 16 hours of right ascension on the celestial sphere. It contains the fourth-brightest star in the night sky, the orange giant Arcturus, which is also the brightest star in the northern celestial hemisphere. Epsilon Bo&ouml;tis, or Izar, is a colourful multiple star popular with amateur astronomers. Bo&ouml;tes is home to many other bright stars.
