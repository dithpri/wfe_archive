Azores Banks are a couple of islands in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean, right south of the Azores archipelago. These islands have hot and dry summers and mild and wet winters. They have a mixture of the &quot;Montado Alentejano&quot; and the North of Portugal. These islands are all socialist republics operating in secret with a lot of languages, but the central language is Kalinio, a Slavic-phonetic language.
