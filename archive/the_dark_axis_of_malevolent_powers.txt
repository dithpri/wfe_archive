Evil comes in many shapes and forms, and has a myriad of goals, ideas and plans, but all are rendered obedient before the Obsidian Throne. The Demon Lord reigns over their infernal legions, their Dark Axis with an iron fist, wrangling the chaotic and quarrelsome forces of evil together that they may oppose their hated foes. The hated Servants of the Goddess, the treacherous Tyrreni, opulent and imperious upon their living isles, and all the rest of the so called &quot;civilized&quot; nations. All will be laid low by the forces of Darkness, and at long last the world shall be returned to its proper state.
