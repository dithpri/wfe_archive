In a hidden part of the world resides a continent that was discovered in 1968. While known to a few, it begins as a nation under fear. The dictatorship of Helena ruled the continent and land. However, since 2016, Boston Mass was born and from it a beautiful democracy uprooted the initial tragedy brought upon this continent. 
The massian continent is a massive land mass with multiple different biomes. Only 3/4 of the continent has been habitated and explored. 
The passage to this mysterious land is treterious and requires one to become lost before it can be found.
