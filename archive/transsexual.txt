A transsexual person is a person who has changed or is in the process of changing their biological sex via medical transition. Transition may include the use of Hormone Replacement Treatment, surgeries such as mastectomy in the case of transsexual men or Facial Feminization Surgery in the case of transsexual women, or sex reassignment surgery.

Despite the use of the same suffix as terms referring to sexuality, such as &quot;homosexual&quot;, or &quot;bisexual&quot;, transsexuality is not a sexual or romantic orientation.
