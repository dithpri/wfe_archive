This region was created to house a number of experimental nations, which will be answering their issues in interesting and diverse ways. With the exception of the Governor, all of these nations are likely to be extreme in ways that, as they mature, will differ quite a lot from one another. Some nations will dismiss most of their issues, answering only those that are likely to foster extreme results of a particular sort. The rest of them will answer most of their issues, and, though they are quite likely to be extreme, it is beyond my ability to predict exactly what forms they will take. If you care, pull up a chair and watch.

See the regional Factbooks, once I have time to write them, to learn more about the strategies of these nations.
