The Concord of Crowns is an ancient alliance of sovereign kingdoms, duchies, principalities, and free cities. Founded during an age of war and uncertainty, the Concord was created upon the belief that no realm should be forced to face its enemies alone.

The affairs of the alliance are governed by the Grand Council, where the rulers and representatives of member states gather to settle disputes, discuss foreign threats, negotiate treaties, and determine matters concerning the common defense.

Its members maintain extensive trade routes, diplomatic ties, and cultural exchanges, allowing merchants, scholars, pilgrims, and nobles to travel between allied lands under the protection of the common oath.
