The Ministry of Cultural Affairs (MoCA) proudly presents the inaugural Pacifica Regional Olympics, a historic celebration of athletic excellence, friendship, and unity across the Commonwealth.

For the first time in our history, nations from every corner of The Pacifica will gather to compete under one banner in a festival of sport inspired by the Olympic spirit. From thrilling track races to aquatic competitions, every event will showcase determination, teamwork, and national pride.

The Games will be hosted by the [nation=New Pleadland], welcoming athletes, officials, and spectators from across the region for six unforgettable days of competition.
