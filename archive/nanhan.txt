Nanhan (&amp;#21335;&amp;#28698;), meaning the &quot;Vast Southern Realm of Waters,&quot; is a powerful maritime empire spanning the tropical peninsulas and emerald archipelagos of the Southern Seas. Founded by successive waves of northern migrants who fled mainland dynastic collapses across the northern continent, Nanhan transformed from a collection of coastal refugee outposts into a dominant seafaring superpower.

Today, it is a land where grand northern architectural symmetries meet tropical monsoon realities, a realm of jade-roofed stilt metropolises, terraced volcanic plantations, and heavily armed treasure junks.
