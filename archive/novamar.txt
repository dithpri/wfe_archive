[color=#4682B4]Welcome to [b]Novamar[/b], where the sparkling waters shine under clear daylight skies and glow with bioluminescence (and industrial chemicals) at night.

Years after the cataclysmic asteroid impacts pummeled Earth, societies reformed and rebuilt themselves. The huge nations of the past are now fractured and split, with very little resemblance to what once was.

From neon-drenched megalopolises to quiet traditional villages, nations and peoples of all cultures and governments live along the shifting, glowing shores.[/color]
