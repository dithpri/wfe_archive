This is the region of [b]Skandilund[/b].

Our skandi nations (united in armed neutrality) show a breathtaking, clean nature with deep fjords, snowcapped mountains and massive glaciers. The weather may be nippy during yuletide, but at least there are reindeer and majestic aurora borealis.

The arctic circle cuts through the northernmost third of the region, and the mighty Skandes mountain range run like a spine from northeast to southwest of the Skandilund peninsula. To the west is the wild nordic sea, and to the east; dark, mist-wreathed forests.

[hr]Being more politically neutral than the colour beige, we [b]do not[/b] accept embassies at this point in time.
