The Pelagic Concordat&#151;formally designated in diplomatic registries as the Eastern Alliance of Devolved States (EADS)&#151;emerged from the systemic fiscal and administrative hollowing of centralized East and Northeast Asian nation-states during the mid-21st-century demographic and supply-chain realignments. Rather than collapsing into warlordism orfailed states, coastal provinces, special economic zones, and island prefectures negotiated asymmetric devolution treaties with their former metropolitan capitals.
