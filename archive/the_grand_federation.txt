[color=#173A78][b]The Grand Federation[/b][/color]

The Grand Federation is a growing pact of sovereign nations built on trust, diplomacy, participation, and long-term cooperation. Every member remains independent, but together we work to build a strong, active, and respected regional community.

Our goal is simple: create a region shaped by its members, supported by reliable institutions, and strengthened through cooperation.
