The Empire of Fortuna is dead, but her legacy echoes through every nation. The world watches with bated breath as two rival factions, both born out of the old empire, vie for power, influence, and the very fate of humanity itself. 
The days of jovial joke cracking in the Imperial parliament are long gone, replaced by the crack of rifles. The hailing and saluting to the Emperor replaced equally by the burning of portraits and the saluting of new idols.
Though the Empire is gone, these nations are nonetheless bound by a shared history and a past of unity; they would sooner die than admit it, but they are all more alike than they are different. Fate will play its hand, one way or another.
