Magnae portas was once a mighty Region, home to tens of nations, all in glorious collaboration. However, one by one, each of them left in pursuit of other things and formed other governments. Eventually, they all focused on the magnificent art of racing, but different. But, held in their hearts, they had a certain nostalgia for it. But they left the glorious region of Magnae portas and focused more on colonizing space, far away from their home system that contained the planet Jord. However, one looked back. One saw the ruins of the once great region, and saw it fallen. They resolved to refound it, and to make it at least exist once more. Long Live Magnae portas!
