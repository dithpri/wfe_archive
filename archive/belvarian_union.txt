Welcome to the Belvarian Union

The Belvarian Union is a peaceful and cooperative region dedicated to democracy, diplomacy, economic prosperity, and mutual respect.

All nations are welcome, whether they are new to NationStates or experienced players. We value friendly cooperation, active participation, and the peaceful resolution of conflicts.

Our shared goals are:

Promote peace and stability.
Encourage trade and economic growth.
Support democracy and national sovereignty.
Build a strong and active international community.
