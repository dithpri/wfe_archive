Snow leopards (scientific name: [i]Panthera uncia[/i]), or sneps, are a species of big cat found in the mountain ranges of Asia. Of the five species of big cats, of the five species in [i]Panthera[/i] genus, they are the smallest. They are also genetically closer to tigers ([i]Panthera tigris[/i]) than true leopards ([i]Panthera pardus[/i]); in addition, they are the only cat of the [i]Panthera[/i] genus that is incapable of roaring.
