THE CONFEDERACY OF CHRISTENDOM

The Confederacy does not demand that its members share a single form of government. Kingdoms, empires, republics, feudal realms, and other Catholic states are welcome, provided that they recognize the sovereignty of fellow members and remain committed to the preservation of Christian civilization.
Membership begins with an Pledge to Christ, formally telegraphed to the Founder and Leader of the Confederacy, Maximillian of St. Maximillian.
