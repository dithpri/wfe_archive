Post-hardcore is a punk rock music genre that maintains the aggression and intensity of hardcore punk but emphasizes a greater degree of creative expression. Like the term &quot;post-punk&quot;, the term &quot;post-hardcore&quot; has been applied to a broad constellation of groups. Initially taking inspiration from post-punk and noise rock, post-hardcore began in the 1980s with bands like H&uuml;sker D&uuml; and Minutemen. The genre expanded in the 1980s and 1990s with releases by bands from cities which had established hardcore scenes, such as Fugazi from Washington, D.C.[6] as well as groups such as Big Black, Jawbox, Quicksand, and Shellac who stuck closer to post-hardcore&#39;s noise rock roots.[6]
