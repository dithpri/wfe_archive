Pisces is a constellation of the zodiac. Its vast bulk &#151; and main asterism viewed in most European cultures per Greco-Roman antiquity as a distant pair of fishes connected by one cord each that join at an apex &#151; are in the Northern celestial hemisphere. Although Pisces is a large constellation, there are only a few stars brighter than magnitude 4 in Pisces: Fumalsamakah, otherwise Beta Piscium (&amp;#946; Psc); Delta Piscium (&amp;#948; Psc); Epsilon Piscium (&amp;#949; Psc); Revati, otherwise Zeta Piscium (&amp;#950; Psc); Torcular, otherwise Omicron Piscium (&amp;#959; Psc); and Omega Piscium (&amp;#969; Psc).
