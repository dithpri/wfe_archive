[b][region]The Island of Charleston[/region][/b] is an island in the Charlesiantic Ocean consisting of three regions, the [b][region]Maschint Alliance[/region][/b], [b][region]The Free United Peoples of Kyrzman[/region][/b] and [b][region]The Great Eastern Fields[/region][/b]. Each region has its own government, however the governor holds authority as a member in the Island of Charleston Security Council.
