The Alaskan Federation is an alliance of 12 independent nations, founded by the Republic of Koyuk Bay and the East Soviet Alaskan territory on January 29th, 2045, to prevent further conflict following the Great Anchorage Wars of 2039-2044, which resulted in hundreds of thousands of deaths and the complete nuclear destruction of Anchorage, Alaska.
