Ursa Major, also known as the Great Bear, is a constellation in the Northern Sky, whose associated mythology likely dates back into prehistory. Ursa Major is primarily known from the asterism of its main seven stars, which has been called the &quot;Big Dipper&quot;, &quot;the Wagon&quot;, &quot;Charles&#39;s Wain&quot;, or &quot;the Plough&quot;, among other names.  Two of its stars, named Dubhe and Merak (&amp;#945; Ursae Majoris and &amp;#946; Ursae Majoris), can be used as the navigational pointer towards the place of the northern pole star, Polaris in Ursa Minor.
