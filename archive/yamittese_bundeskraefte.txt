The Yamittese Bundeskraefte (lit. &#39;Federal Forces&#39;) are the armed forces of [nation=long]Yamitt[/nation]. The Bundeskraefte consists of three armed forces: the Yamittese Army, the Yamittese Navy, and the Yamittese Air Force.
