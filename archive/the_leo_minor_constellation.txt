Leo Minor is a small and faint constellation in the northern celestial hemisphere. Its name is Latin for &quot;the smaller lion&quot;, in contrast to Leo, the larger lion. It lies between the larger and more recognizable Ursa Major to the north and Leo to the south. There are 37 stars brighter than apparent magnitude 6.5 in the constellation; three are brighter than magnitude 4.5. 46 Leonis Minoris, an orange giant of magnitude 3.8, is located some 95 light-years from Earth. At magnitude 4.4, Beta Leonis Minoris is the second-brightest star and the only one with a Bayer designation. The third-brightest star is 21 Leonis Minoris.
