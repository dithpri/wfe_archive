The Time Gentry are an ancient race of extraterrestrial people who hail from the planet Gallifrey and are stated to have invented time travel technology. They have sworn an oath to not interfere in the universe; those who reject this and leave the planet to live in the universe are referred to as &quot;bounders&quot;. 

One of their number, William Hartnell, fled Gallifrey, stealing one of their time machines known as a TASCOR.

The Denizens of The Time Gentry are often reproached for their recalcitrance and impertinence.
