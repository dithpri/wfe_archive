Welcome to the Verlian Commonwealth!

The Commonwealth brings together Verlian territories and associated governments to make regional cooperation a little easier.

Members keep their own governments and institutions while working together on shared issues like infrastructure, environmental policy, development, and other regional projects.

It gives the wider Verlian system a common place to coordinate without taking away the individual identity or internal government of each member.
