[color=#D4AF37][b]THE WOVEN WORLD[/b][/color]

[i]A world of powder, steel, sorcery, and ruin.[/i]

[b]Genre:[/b] Low Fantasy Baroque Era

[u]Overview[/u]

The Woven World is a low-fantasy setting divided between rival kingdoms, empires, republics, and ancient realms. Most nations rely upon pike-and-shot armies, cavalry, black-powder artillery, fortified cities, and wooden sailing fleets.

Magic comes from the [b]Weave[/b], an unseen force that permeates the world but remains poorly understood. Centuries ago, a great calamity destroyed much of civilization&#146;s knowledge and corrupted countless living beings, creating the creatures collectively known as the [b]Nocturne[/b].
