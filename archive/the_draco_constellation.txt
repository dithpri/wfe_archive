Draco is a constellation in the far northern sky. Its name is derived from the Latin word for dragon. Thuban (&amp;#945; Draconis) was the northern pole star from 3942 BC, when it moved farther north than Theta Bo&ouml;tis, until 1793 BC. There are three stars above magnitude 3 in Draco. The brighter of the three, and the brightest star in Draco, is Gamma Draconis, traditionally called Etamin or Eltanin. Nearby Beta Draconis, traditionally called Rastaban, is a yellow giant star of magnitude 2.8, 362 light-years from Earth.
