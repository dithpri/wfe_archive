Eridanus is a constellation which stretches along the southern celestial hemisphere. It is represented as a river. At its southern end is the magnitude 0.5 star Achernar, designated Alpha Eridani. There are several other noteworthy stars in Eridanus, including some double stars: Beta Eridani, traditionally called Cursa; Theta Eridani, called Acamar; Epsilon Eridani (Ran) is a star with one extrasolar planet similar to Jupiter.
