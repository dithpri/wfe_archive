Scorpius is a zodiac constellation located in the Southern celestial hemisphere, where it sits near the center of the Milky Way, between Libra to the west and Sagittarius to the east. Scorpius contains many bright stars, including Antares (&amp;#945; Sco), &quot;rival of Mars,&quot; so named because of its distinct reddish hue; &amp;#946;1 Sco (Graffias or Acrab), a triple star; &amp;#948; Sco (Dschubba, &quot;the forehead&quot;); &amp;#952; Sco (Sargas, of Sumerian origin); &amp;#957; Sco (Jabbah); &amp;#958; Sco; &amp;#960; Sco (Fang); &amp;#963; Sco (Alniyat); and &amp;#964; Sco (Paikauhale).
