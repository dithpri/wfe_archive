Umpafor unites three sovereign nations through trust and cooperation; internal hostility is virtually nonexistent.

[b]Tokaniko[/b] is famed for compassion and hospitality: &#147;These people are impossible to hate, and impossible to have hate.&#148; Its economy reportedly exceeds the combined output of every other country. Its military institutions report no task failures.

[b]Montendigo[/b] is known for reliable electricity, excellent education and public security. Its economy includes tourism, agriculture and woodchip exports.

[b]Lumacon[/b] is renowned for woodworking, craftsmanship, engineering, utilities, firefighting and rainforests.

All three maintain compulsory military service and cooperate in trade, industry and defence.
