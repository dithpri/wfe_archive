The Glorious Alliance for Allied Nations was created from a 2026 pact by the nations of Mercixia and Uxumiad, who after gaining independence realised the world&#39;s hostility to these two glorious founding nations, and created a region for nations like themselves to truly assert themselves.

This region is fiercely independent and known for its ultranationalism, skepticism of foreign influence, and isolationism from the World Assembly, only rarely voting.

An odd mix of ultranationalism and cooperation, The Glorious Alliance for Allied Nations will certainly prosper for years to come.
