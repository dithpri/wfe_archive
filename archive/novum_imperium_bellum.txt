Novum Imperium Bellum is a highly militarized, sovereign coalition of nations under an emperor nation. Established as a bastion for martial excellence, strategic defense, and industrial dominance, the region stands as a disciplined collective of global powers. While internal sovereignty is fiercely guarded by individual member states, the region is unified under a singular ruler and its unbreakable doctrine: peace through overwhelming strength.
