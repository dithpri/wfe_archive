The Dwivdei Empire was one that formed out of the strife out of nuclear apocalypse and founded by the Dwivdei dynasty from a pre-apocalyptic empire from the south of gargantuan size. Dwivdei is the only known large-scale civilisation, with smaller and unnoticeable city-states to the north and to the south,, in between irradiated zones, although legends tell of a land in Caucasia. The Dwivdei empire spans the entire Himalayan Area and has a sphere of influence spanning from India to China.
