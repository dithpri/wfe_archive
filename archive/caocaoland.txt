Welcome to Caocaoland, a confectionary paradise where rich rivers of melted dark chocolate wind through rolling hills of truffles and towering cocoa-bean forests. Established by sweet-toothed leaders and master chocolatiers, this NationStates region thrives on a bustling economy of artisanal sweets, diplomatic cocoa trades, and sugar-fueled innovation. Whether your nation is a strict, high-percentage dark chocolate empire or a laid-back, creamy milk-chocolate republic, Caocaoland offers a decadent home for every flavor of governance.
