[color=#de5555]The Strait of Hormuz is a waterway between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman. It provides the only sea passage from the Persian Gulf to the ocean and is one of the world&#39;s most important choke points. It is a major route of petroleum products for Europe and Asia and is critical for Europe&#39;s energy supply. It is also the only maritime route for several Gulf countries including the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Iraq, and disruption to the strait can cause severe supply shortages.
