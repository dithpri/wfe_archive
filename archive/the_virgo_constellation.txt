Virgo is one of the constellations of the zodiac. The name means &quot;maiden&quot; in Latin. Besides Spica, other bright stars in Virgo include &amp;#946; Virginis (Zavijava), &amp;#947; Virginis (Porrima), &amp;#948; Virginis (Auva) and &amp;#949; Virginis (Vindemiatrix). Other fainter stars that were also given names are &amp;#950; Virginis (Heze), &amp;#951; Virginis (Zaniah), &amp;#953; Virginis (Syrma), &amp;#954; Virginis (Kang), &amp;#955; Virginis (Khambalia) and &amp;#966; Virginis (Elgafar).
