BoC is a NationStates region for political discussion and writing laws or policies. Every once in a while, we discuss issues or topics and vote on a resolution.

Are we a copy of the World Assembly? No. Unlike the WA, where anyone can vote, only verified Citizens have the authority to participate in elections. The BoC is a serious platform for consensus and we do not care about condemning or endorsing fake nations. This is a place where people are brought together. This is a digital emulation of an anarchist cafe, where people gather to discuss relevant matters that will be brought to action in reality.

Please read our rules after joining.
