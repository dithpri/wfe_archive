Rising from shores choked with ash and oil-slicked tides, The Industrial Isle is a sprawling, soot-stained dominion of roaring blast furnaces, towering gantry cranes, and endless lines of heavy-duty assembly plants. This is a landscape built entirely around raw production, where the air hums with the permanent vibration of heavy turbines and the ground shakes beneath a relentless churn of industrial waste and high-heat manufacturing.
&amp;#8203;The furnaces burn hot, the assembly lines are moving, and the region stands ready for new nations to bring their manufacturing and industry to help grow the Industrial Isles to our ultimate potential.
