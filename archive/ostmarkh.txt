Ostmarkh is a ravaged section of the world, being dubbed as a &#39;graveyard of empires&#39;, due to numerous foreign powers having attempted to invade and colonise the region, all subsequently having collosal failures.

It&#39;s most notable feature is the wide range of mountains within the area along with a giant abundance of iron and titanium. Growing anything here is nigh impossible, other than underground due to a relentless nuclear winter in the region.

Struggling to survive and having nowhere else to go, the people of Ostmarkh keep pushing for the hopes of a better future.

The region is currently being protected from foreign powers by Obhyzka and Wedisnest.
