Many Worlds is a large scale advanced space age Universe split into several of its own dimensions. The dimensions or worlds are; The Observable Universe, Unobservable Universe, The Broken, Sheol, The Empty, The Void and The Divide. With the possibility of another dimension being added. The only ones that are actually inhabited are the Observable and Unobservable Universe, known more simply as the Material World and Immaterial World. There is only one Universe in Many Worlds, the Multiverse does not exist.  Hopefully I can add more details to this later
