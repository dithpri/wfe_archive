The ADAC, officially the Allgemeiner Deutscher Automobil-Club (lit.&amp;#8201;&#39;General German Automobile Club&#39;), is Europe&#39;s largest automobile association. The ADAC is the largest Verein (club) in Germany, with around 21 million members. Its headquarters are located in Munich. Its original and most well-known service is roadside assistance. The objective of the ADAC is &quot;the representation, promotion, and advocacy of motoring, motorsport, and tourism interests.&quot;
