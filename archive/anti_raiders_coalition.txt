The Anti-Raiders Coalition (ARC) is a dedicated defender organization in NationStates committed to protecting regions and preserving the stability of local communities.
By deploying rapid-response tactical units and coordinating with regional founders and World Assembly delegates, ARC works to liberate invaded territories, repel unprovoked raids, and safeguard regional sovereignty. Whether your community is facing an active threat or looking to strengthen its long-term border security, ARC provides the military support, intelligence, and strategic guidance needed to keep your region safe.
