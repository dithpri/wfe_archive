Membership to this particular region is open and voluntary to all CCC members. There is no requirement to join, no mandatory activity level, and no political agenda beyond the shared interest in serious and historically grounded Christian roleplay. Nations may be monarchies, republics, theocracies, or constitutional states. What unites members is not uniformity of governance or doctrine but a common commitment to portraying their nation&#39;s religious life with the depth and authenticity that the canonical Christian traditions deserve.
