The Wangchen Commonwealth is a composite intergovernmental organisation founded and led by Matila, open to all sovereign nations worldwide that submit voluntary applications for membership.

It incorporates two major functional pillars:
 
1.&nbsp;Integration &amp; Cooperation Pillar 
Deep-level economic and trade connectivity among memeber states, free movement of people,unified regional      standards,cross-border scientific research collaboration and cultural integraton. A commonwealth joint deliberative 
body is established to negotiate regional regulations on livelihood,commerece and technology.
2.&nbsp;Collective Security Pillar 
When a member state suffers external armed aggression, the Commonwealth may launch coordinated response.
