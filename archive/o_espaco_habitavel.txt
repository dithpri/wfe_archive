O Espa&ccedil;o Habit&aacute;vel (The Living Space) Is a nation brought into existence on the European continent by sheer force by H 69 years ago, in 2036. It is, for the most part, a relatively isolationist, and nigh-anarchic country. It resides in the land that used to be mainland France before it was wiped out by its hyper-powered landlord: H. This region serves as an emphasis on the borders already put in place by The Living Space between it and the rest of the world.
