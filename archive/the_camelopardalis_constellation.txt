Camelopardalis is a large but faint constellation of the Northern Sky representing a giraffe. Although Camelopardalis is the 18th largest constellation, it is not a particularly bright constellation, as the brightest stars are only of fourth magnitude. In fact, it only contains four stars brighter than magnitude 5.0. &amp;#946; Cam is the brightest star in Camelopardalis with an apparent magnitude of 4.03.
