The&nbsp;FIA Formula 2 Championship&nbsp;(F2) is a second tier&nbsp;single-seater&nbsp;championship organised by the&nbsp;F&eacute;d&eacute;ration Internationale de l&#39;Automobile. The championship was introduced in 2017, following the rebranding of the long-term&nbsp;Formula One&nbsp;feeder series&nbsp;GP2. The series original founders were&nbsp;Flavio Briatore&nbsp;and current managing director&nbsp;Bruno Michel. Although it is not necessary, most current F1 drivers have previously participated in Formula 2 or GP2 before graduating to Formula One. It is the final step on the&nbsp;FIA Global Pathway&nbsp;driver development ladder.
