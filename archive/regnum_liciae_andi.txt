Regnum Liciae-Andi is a small kingdom within the Potonan Channel comprising mainly the two isles of Lisia and Andium, along with smaller islands, and were originally kingdoms under Romano-Viking settlers until Abito&#39;s invasions, which displaced the old Romano-Norse religion and language, and were gifted as an independent vassal kingdom to Iullius Abito&#39;s friend Ennius Balbus, whose descendants presided over the collapse of civilisation and the liberation of the Kingdom of Lisia-Andium, whose main export is illicit drugs and agricultural products not available in the northern giant of Potona.
