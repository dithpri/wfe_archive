The Eternal Concord
The Eternal Concord is a sovereign community of nations founded upon cooperation, independence, tradition, and mutual respect. Its members share a commitment to maintaining peace while preserving the unique identities and cultures of their nations.

The Concord welcomes nations seeking a stable international community without requiring them to surrender their sovereignty. Through diplomacy, commerce, and collective action, its members work toward a prosperous and enduring future.
