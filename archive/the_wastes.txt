[color=A58B48]A region built by its nations, for its nations.[/color]

[color=34536A]The Wastes is a community of nations united by shared ambition, collective strength, and the pursuit of greatness. We believe that a region is only as strong as the nations that comprise it, and that its success is built through the contributions, achievements, and cooperation of its members.[/color]

[color=34536A]Here, nations are encouraged to develop, leaders are given opportunities to rise, and every member has a stake in the future of the region. Through activity, loyalty, and mutual cooperation, we seek to build something greater than any one nation could achieve alone.[/color]
