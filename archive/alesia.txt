[color=#153A5B][b]THE COMMONWEALTH[/b][/color]

Founded in 2022, Alesia is an independent Commonwealth of nations united by the principles of liberty, sovereignty, and mutual respect. We champion free expression, meaningful diplomacy, and a close-knit community where every nation has a voice. Our strength lies not in numbers, but in the character of our people and the principles we uphold.
