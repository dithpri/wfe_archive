Region of the small lonely island state of Potona, last hold-out of the Latin armies. This one founded by legionaries under the control of Abito. Potona is the only known nation to have survived nuclear warfare, suffering only two strikes on the Abitoite Capital of Londinium and Camulodonum, the second largest city within the entirety of the isles.

Trading outposts and religious missions exist in Islandia (Sinusfumi) and Norcum (Portus Septentrionalis) but settlements thus far have resided within the isles of Britannia and Hibernia, due to the appalling state of the economy due to nuclear warfare. Current capital is Eboracum, current dictator is Titus Calavius Abito.
