Region of the two small island nations of Potona and the even smaller island nation of Lisia-Andium, last hold-outs of the Latin armies, Potona falling under the direct room of Abito and Lisia-Andium under the vassal king Bantius. Both surviving nuclear war with strikes on on the two largest cities in Potona. Current capitals are Eboracum (Potona) and Andium (Lisia-Andium)

Trading outposts and religious missions exist in Islandia (Sinusfumi) and Norcum (Portus Septentrionalis) but settlements thus far have resided within the isles of Britannia and Hibernia, due to the appalling state of the economy due to nuclear warfare. Current capital is Eboracum, current dictator is Titus Calavius Abito and the Rex of Lisia-Andium is Aulus Bantius Nasica
