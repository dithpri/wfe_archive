[b]Pink Lady[/b] &amp;#12500;&amp;#12531;&amp;#12463;&#149;&amp;#12524;&amp;#12487;&amp;#12451;&amp;#12540; is a Japanese duet group. Members are Mitsuyo Nemoto (Mie) and Keiko Masuda (Kei), formed in 1976. Their known songs are [b]&amp;#12459;&amp;#12523;&amp;#12513;&amp;#12531;&#39;77[/b] (Carmen&#39;77),  [b]&amp;#28186;&amp;#12398;&amp;#12471;&amp;#12531;&amp;#12489;&amp;#12496;&amp;#12483;&amp;#12489;[/b], [b]UFO[/b], [b]Southpaw/&amp;#12469;&amp;#12454;&amp;#12473;&amp;#12509;&amp;#12540;[/b], and many more, and even reaching minor success in the United States. They disbanded in 1981, and many reattempts were made ever since, but were not very successful.
