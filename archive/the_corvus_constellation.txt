Corvus is a small constellation in the Southern Celestial Hemisphere. Its name means &quot;raven&quot; in Latin. The four brightest stars, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon, and Beta Corvi, form a distinctive quadrilateral or cross-shape in the night sky. With an apparent magnitude of 2.59, Gamma Corvi&#151;also known as Gienah&#151;is the brightest star in the constellation.
