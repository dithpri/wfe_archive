[color=goldenrod][b][u]Overview[/u][/b][/color]
[color=maroon]The [b]Empire of the Romans[/b] (&amp;#914;&amp;#945;&amp;#963;&amp;#953;&amp;#955;&amp;#949;&amp;#943;&amp;#945; &amp;#964;&amp;#8182;&amp;#957; &amp;#8172;&amp;#969;&amp;#956;&amp;#945;&amp;#943;&amp;#969;&amp;#957;, [i]Basile&iacute;a t&amp;#333;n Rh&amp;#333;ma&iacute;&amp;#333;n[/i]), is a Byzantine/Eastern Roman region for those who seek to revive the grandeur of Constantinople, the splendor of Hagia Sophia, and the indomitable spirit of Rome in the East.[/color]

[list]
[*][color=maroon][b]Basileus[/b] &#150; Supreme sovereign of the Empire.
