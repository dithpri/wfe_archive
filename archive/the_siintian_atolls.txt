A tropical region in the East Pacific, home to a magnificent geographic network of coral rings, pristine tropical islands, and deeply fortified maritime territories.  These nations are bound together by shared lineage, a mutual defence pact, and the unified prefix of our founding societies; these Atolls represent the pinnacle of Pacific sovereignty.
