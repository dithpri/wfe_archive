Monoceros (Greek: &amp;#924;&amp;#959;&amp;#957;&amp;#972;&amp;#954;&amp;#949;&amp;#961;&amp;#969;&amp;#962;, &quot;unicorn&quot;) is a faint constellation on the celestial equator. Monoceros contains only a few fourth magnitude stars, making it difficult to see with the naked eye. Alpha Monocerotis has a visual magnitude of 3.93, while for Gamma Monocerotis it is 3.98.
