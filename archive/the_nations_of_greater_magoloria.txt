Welcome to The Nations of Greater Magoloria, a group of nations located in the Western U.S. We are a relatively small region(but growing!), and are willing to accept nations into our region. If you want to join, then feel free to join The Nations of Greater Magoloria anytime! 

[b]Why Join The Nations of Greater Magoloria?
[/b]
Greater Magoloria is a safe haven for smaller nations. Compared to other regions, it&#39;s quite compact! You can interact with other nations, within Greater Magoloria. You can roleplay with other nations in Greater Magoloria, start alliances(or not, your choice), trade resources, and meet with the others.

Even if it is compact and tiny, it&#39;s a nice place to hang out and talk!
