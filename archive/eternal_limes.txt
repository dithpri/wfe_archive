The Realm of the Eternal Lime: Evergreen Isle is a mythical island drifting in the southern oceans, known for its lush lime groves, gentle wildlife, and eternal spring climate. Home to the Limekeepers, who celebrate unique traditions and speak Limeric, the isle is famous for its artistry, crafts, and luck-bringing Zeststones&#151;though the legendary fruit is never exported. Only the adventurous can find this shifting, enchanting land, where wonder endures with every tide.
