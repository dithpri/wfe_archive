Veyra is a sector of space in which the Empire of Amiarce and its homeworld, Terra, are located.

It is the broader astronomical and geographical region associated with Amiarce&#39;s interplanetary territory, political influence, and military activities.

Terra serves as the ancestral homeworld of the Amiarce Empire, while Solora serves as its imperial capital and principal seat of national government.

Veyra is therefore a region of considerable importance to Amiarce, containing the Empire&#39;s political center and the origins of its civilization.

The sector is relevant to the Empire&#39;s military, economic, diplomatic, and interplanetary interests.
