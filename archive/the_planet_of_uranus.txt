Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. It is a gaseous cyan-coloured ice giant. Most of the planet is made of water, ammonia, and methane in a supercritical phase of matter, which astronomers call &quot;ice&quot; or &quot;volatiles&quot;. The planet&#39;s atmosphere has a complex layered cloud structure, and has the lowest minimum temperature (49 K (&amp;#8722;224 &deg;C; &amp;#8722;371 &deg;F)) of all of the Solar System&#39;s planets. It has a marked axial tilt of 82.23&deg;, with a retrograde rotation period of 17 hours and 14 minutes. This means that in an 84-Earth-year orbital period around the Sun, its poles get around 42 years of continuous sunlight, followed by 42 years of continuous darkness.
