Official Ideology: Theocratic Mixed-Market Republicanism
Motto: &quot;Through Faith, Industry; For All, Prosperity.&quot;
Government &amp; Politics
&#149; System: Theocratic Republic. Power is shared between a democratically elected General Assembly and a religious council called the High Synod.
&#149; The Capitalist Aspect: Free enterprise is highly encouraged. Citizens are free to start businesses, trade globally, and generate private wealth. Profits are viewed as a divine blessing.
&#149; The Socialist Aspect: High corporate taxes fund a universal welfare system. The state owns critical infrastructure (healthcare, energy, transit) to ensure no citizen falls into poverty.
