The Organization of Free Nations (OFN) is a major political and military coalition comprised primarily of democratic states, spearheaded and led by the United States of American Capitalism, governed under American hegemony and primarily headquartered in Washington, D.C.

Geopolitical Role: The OFN operates under a &quot;Roll Back Doctrine&quot;, systematically finances, trains, and backs separatist groups, rebel factions, and democratic movements inside the spheres of its totalitarian rivals.
