Located on the west side of the Karanic Sea, Kamaranu is home to the nations of Karanu on the east coast, Amarania to the west, and the Community of Amaranoa on the Amaranoan Isle.

The land is mostly occupied by forests and the occasional inland plains, and while the climate is generally temperate, taigas rest on its northernmost edges, with the Amaranoan Isle being completely covered in taigas.

Recently, there has been reports of several previously missing individuals turning up dead, often without any clear cause, in and around a particular forest in Southwest Amarania. Investigations are underway.
