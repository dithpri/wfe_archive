The Faroe Union is a collection of island states in the North Atlantic Ocean above the Faroe Islands and west of Norway. It&#39;s most notable members are The Leo Islands, The Lucas Islands, Bensman and the North and South Avacado Lands. Fun fact, the name Avacado in Avacado land isn&#39;t named after the food, but it&#39;s actually after Queen Avacado I, the leader of the Fartein Union.
