Jeffrey David Sachs (born November 5, 1954) is an American economist and public policy scholar. A professor at Columbia University, he formerly directed The Earth Institute and currently leads the Center for Sustainable Development. Sachs has held prominent leadership roles within the United Nations, serving as Special Adviser to the Secretary-General (2002&#150;2018), President of the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, co-founder of the Millennium Promise Alliance, and a key architect of the MDGs and SDGs. A prolific author and influential policy thinker, his perspectives on economics, the origins of COVID-19, and the Russian invasion of Ukraine have attracted both widespread recognition and sustained controversy.
