Silent Outsider is a region with only one nation: Dystopia Reimagined.

Quiet, independent, and deliberately distant from the rest of the world, Silent Outsider exists beyond regional politics and unnecessary alliances. Its sole nation governs itself without interference, watching the world from afar while remaining largely unheard.

There are no crowds, no rival governments, and no unnecessary complications. Just one nation, one region, and silence.
