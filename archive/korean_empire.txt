The [color=navy][b]Korean Empire[/b][/color] was a monarchical state that existed on the Korean Peninsula from 1897 to 1910. It was established by [nation]Emperor Gojong[/nation] with the aim of breaking free from the influence of the traditional tributary system of the Qing Dynasty and establishing [region]Korea[/region] as an independent empire.

Owned by the Republic of [region]Korea[/region]
