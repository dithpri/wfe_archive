A loose confederation headed by the major superpowers of the Arcanial world: United Socialist States of Sonvia, Armed Republic of Notrus and Kingdom of Great Frast. 

The Confederation of Arcanial Nations (CAN) aims to build a cooperative, inclusive and unified international community throughout the world, no matter what ideology or belief which sets countries apart. It offers joint sessions, commissions, and agencies to reach this goal.

It meets to discuss in rotating locations: 
Sol Vetz Grand Arcanial Nations Convention Center, Sol Vetz Tet, Sonvia
Novex Grand Arcanial Nations Convention Center, Notrus
Vancross Grand Arcanial Nations Convention Center, Great Frast
