The Mesoamerican Treaty Organization (MTO) consists of the states of Guadalopoltepec, Cemaxcatltepec, Tlakaxoxoukayotltepec and Hueyitlamachticallitepec, all sharing the continent of Mesoamerica. It is a multinational alliance between four distinctive ideological states to deter regional conflict and prevent influences from outside Mesoamerica. All MTO member states have a common currency called the quachtli.
