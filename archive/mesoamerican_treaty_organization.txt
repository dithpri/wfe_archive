The Mesoamerican Treaty Organization (MTO) consists of the states of Cemaxcatltepec, Chicahualiztlitepec, Guadalopoltepec, Hueyitlamachticallitepec, Tlakaxoxoukayotltepec, Tlamachilistlitepec and Yolyamanictepec, all sharing the continent of Mesoamerica. It is a multinational alliance between seven distinctive ideological states to deter regional conflict and prevent influences from outside Mesoamerica. All MTO member states have a common currency called the quachtli.
