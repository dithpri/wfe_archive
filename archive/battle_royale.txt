[b][color=red]Battle Royale (Japanese: &amp;#12496;&amp;#12488;&amp;#12523;&amp;#12539;&amp;#12525;&amp;#12527;&amp;#12452;&amp;#12450;&amp;#12523;, Hepburn: Batoru Rowaiaru) is a Japanese dystopian horror novel by journalist Koushun Takami. The book tells the story of junior high school students who are forced to fight each other to the death in a program run by a fictional fascist, totalitarian Japanese government known as the Republic of Greater East Asia.

In 2000, one year after publication, Battle Royale was adapted into a manga series, written by Takami himself, and a feature film. The film was both controversial and successful, becoming one of the year&#39;s highest-grossing films as well as prompting condemnation by Japan&#39;s National Diet.
