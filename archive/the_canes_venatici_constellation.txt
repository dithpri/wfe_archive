Canes Venatici is a small northern constellation that was created by Johannes Hevelius in the 17th century. Its name is Latin for &#39;hunting dogs&#39;, and the constellation is often depicted in illustrations as representing the dogs of Bo&ouml;tes the Herdsman. Cor Caroli is the constellation&#39;s brightest star, with an apparent magnitude of 2.9. La Superba (Y CVn) is one of the reddest naked-eye stars and one of the brightest carbon stars.
