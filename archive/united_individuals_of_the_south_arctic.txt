United Individuals Of The South Arctic

Core Principles: Individualism, national independence, and regional cooperation.

Membership: Nations must pass the official government screening process before admission.

Security: The regional password is confidential and must not be shared with other nations. Prospective members must contact the Governor to begin the official screening process.

Government Officers:
Governor: The Commonwealth of Snowlantis
Security Officer: The Republic of Eksan
Home Land Officer: The Empire of Kolomaya

National Objective: To encourage individualistic goals while maintaining an organized, secure, and cooperative regional community.
