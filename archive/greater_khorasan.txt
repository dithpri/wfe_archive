Greater Khorasan is a mountainous arid region in modern day northeast Iran, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. It has historically been under the control of Persians and Mongols, such as the Achaemenid, Parthian, Sasanian, Safavid, Afsharid, Samanid, Chagatai, Timurid, and Mongol empires.
