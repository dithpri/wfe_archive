Phoenix is a minor constellation in the southern sky. The brightest star, Alpha Phoenicis, is named Ankaa, an Arabic word meaning &#39;the Phoenix&#39;. It is an orange giant of apparent magnitude 2.4. Next is Beta Phoenicis, actually a binary system composed of two yellow giants with a combined apparent magnitude of 3.3. Nu Phoenicis has a dust disk, while the constellation has ten star systems with known planets and the recently discovered galaxy clusters El Gordo and the Phoenix Cluster&#151;located 7.2 and 5.7 billion light years away respectively, two of the largest objects in the visible universe.
