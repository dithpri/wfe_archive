[hr]

The [b][region]Maschint Alliance[/region][/b] is one of three regions on [region]The Island of Charleston[/region], along with [b][region]The Great Eastern Fields[/region][/b] and [b][region]The Free United Peoples of Kyrzman[/region][/b]. The Maschint Alliance is working on creating a new, prosperous environment for nations and to bring our people to international glory. 

[hr]

The Maschint Alliance is currently creating a haven for sport, with new football leagues and cups being made. The [b]Charlesian Superleague[/b] is the major league in the Maschint Alliance, consisting of the best teams in [b][nation]Greater Charles[/nation], [nation]Great Charleston[/nation], [nation]Cloudembourg[/nation] and [nation]Southern Jamestown[/nation][/b].
