The [region]Maschint Alliance[/region] is one of three regions on [region]The Island of Charleston[/region], along with [region]The Great Eastern Fields[/region] and [region]The Free United Peoples of Kyrzman[/region]. The Maschint Alliance is working on creating a new, prosperous environment for nations and to bring our people to international glory. 

[hr]

The Maschint Alliance is currently creating a haven for sport, with new football leagues and cups being made. The Charlesian Superleague is the major league in the Maschint Alliance, consisting of the best teams in [nation]Greater Charles[/nation], [nation]Great Charleston[/nation], [nation]Cloudembourg[/nation] and [nation]Southern Jamestown[/nation].
