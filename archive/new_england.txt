[color=#B22234][b]New England[/b][/color] is a historic and vibrant community consisting of six states: Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and Connecticut. United by the values of liberty, unity, and self-governance, we embrace our rich heritage while striving for progress and prosperity. All nations are welcome to join us in building a strong and enduring community.
