Horologium is a constellation of six stars faintly visible in the southern celestial hemisphere. The constellation&#39;s brightest star&#151;and the only one brighter than an apparent magnitude of 4&#151;is Alpha Horologii (at 3.85), an aging orange giant star that has swollen to around 11 times the diameter of the Sun. The long-period variable-brightness star, R Horologii (4.7 to 14.3), has one of the largest variations in brightness among all stars in the night sky visible to the unaided eye. Four star systems in the constellation are known to have exoplanets.
