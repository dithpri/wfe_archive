Volans is a constellation in the southern sky. It represents a flying fish; its name is a shortened form of its original name, Piscis Volans. There are two double stars within the constellation which can be observed using a small telescope, Gamma Volantis and Epsilon Volantis.The magnitudes of the Gamma Volantis stars are 3.8 and 5.6, and of Epsilon Volantis 4.4 and 7.3
