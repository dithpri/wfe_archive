&amp;#128081; KINGDOM OF BAVUVA

Government: Absolute Monarchy
Head of State: The King
Economic System: Capitalism
Administration: Centralized Royal Government

&amp;#128081; The Crown

The King holds supreme authority over Bavuva and makes all final national decisions. He appoints and dismisses all department heads.

&amp;#127963;&amp;#65039; Royal Departments

Bavuva has five departments:

1. Department of Finance
2. Department of Economy
3. Department of Defence
4. Department of Foreign Affairs
5. Department of Industry &amp; Infrastructure

Each department head independently recruits and manages their personnel, subject to the King&#39;s authority.

&amp;#128176; National Ambition

To establish Bavuva as a leading global capitalist economy, industrial powerhouse and military superpower.
