The [b]Cretaceous[/b] is a geological period that lasted from about [b]143.1 to 66 Ma[/b] (million years ago). It is the third and final period of the [b]Mesozoic Era[/b], as well as the longest...The name is derived from the Latin creta, &#39;chalk&#39;, which is abundant in deposits from the latter half of the period. It is usually abbreviated [b]K[/b], for its German translation Kreide.

The Cretaceous was a period with a relatively warm climate, resulting in high eustatic sea levels that created numerous shallow inland seas. These oceans and seas were populated with now-extinct marine reptiles, ammonites and rudists; while on land, dinosaurs continued to dominate.
