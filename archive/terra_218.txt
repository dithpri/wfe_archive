Welcome to Terra 218, an alternate Earth fractured into competing empires, kingdoms, republics and city-states.

You have arrived in a world whose future has not yet been written.

Build your nation, establish alliances, negotiate treaties and compete for territory through agreed games, military campaigns and duels.

Every conquest, defeat and diplomatic settlement becomes part of our shared history.

[b]No Demon Lord. No predetermined victor.[/b]

All player conflicts are voluntary, with agreed stakes and rules.
