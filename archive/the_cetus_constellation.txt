Cetus is a constellation, sometimes called &#39;the whale&#39; in English. In Greek mythology, the Cetus was a large sea monster which both Perseus and Heracles needed to slay. Beta Ceti is the brightest star in the constellation of Cetus. It is officially named Diphda. Alpha Ceti is the second-brightest star in the constellation of Cetus. Its official name is Menkar.
