A region for simulating the territories of an alternate timeline[nation]Aurorasen[/nation]. 

At the end of Second World War, an operation to dismantle the communist union and storm the imperialists in the east was enacted. This aggression only spiraled exponentially until the great flash occurred and the world stood still. The atomic bombs were new, concentrated, yet horrifying and fathomable in their destruction. What arose in the aftermath was madness, first in the form of disorder then in the form of tyranny. Aurorasen managed to remain relatively unscathed from the blasts and in the ensuing rise of totalitarian regimes from the ashes, it became the desperate beacon of liberty and egalitarianism.
