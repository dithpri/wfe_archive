[b]Doppler-Sonar[/b] is a landmass and island that replaces Namibia, Botswana, and the entirety of Southern Africa. it is seperated from mainland Africa via river deltas, straits and other bodies of water, known as the [b]Namibian System[/b]. it is full of Bantustans.
