Industrial music (or simply industrial) is a subgenre of experimental music that emerged in the mid-to late 1970s, primarily inspired by industrial society, while drawing influences from avant-garde and early electronic music genres such as musique concr&egrave;te, tape music, noise and sound collage.[1] The term was coined in 1976 by Monte Cazazza and adopted by Throbbing Gristle with the founding of Industrial Records.
