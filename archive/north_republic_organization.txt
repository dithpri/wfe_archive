Founded on March 12, 2026, the North Republic Organization (NRO) is an intergovernmental association dedicated to preserving democratic governance, free elections, and civil liberties. Maintained through a non-partisan framework that remains neutral on economic models and partisan politics, the NRO focuses strictly on procedural safeguards, including independent judicial oversight, constitutional checks and balances, and universal franchise.

Membership is open to any sovereign state or region that upholds economic principles.
